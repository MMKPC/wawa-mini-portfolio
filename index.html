Dense search found one additional relevant source. OR search ranked the first relevant result better and ran much faster. This is an exploratory fixture, with no independent human validation.

01 One question, two paths.

Persistent keyword search

SQLite FTS5 indexes document passages. Explicit AND and OR conditions make the matching policy visible and provide a strong baseline.

Local semantic retrieval

Ollama supplies Nomic embeddings. A persisted 768-dimensional snapshot is searched with exact cosine similarity.

Evidence before claims

Ranked source passages, automated checks, and recorded evaluation results make the behavior inspectable. Answer generation remains future work.
